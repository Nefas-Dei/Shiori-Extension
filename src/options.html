How it works

The extension only sends a progress entry when it is at least as far as the locally known position. The server keeps the highest updated_at, so a browser tab left open before you read on your phone is rejected with 409 and returns the newer entry instead.

Percentage (0–100) and paragraph index are stored alongside the chapter number, not pixel scroll values, so positions remain meaningful across desktop and mobile layouts.
